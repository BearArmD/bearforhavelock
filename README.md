# Bear for Havelock

Campaign website for Harlan "Bear" McGillem — candidate for **Havelock City Commissioner** (November 3, 2026). Built and maintained by the candidate. Live at **https://bearforhavelock.com**.

## About

A lifelong Havelock native running on three plain commitments: enforce the ordinances already on the books, get commercial truck traffic off residential roads, and answer every constituent. No committee, no machine, no donors — self-funded and self-built.

## Read the record

I don't ask Havelock to trust my word — I ask you to read the record. The `/Records` folder holds the primary documents behind the claims on the site:

- **FOIA-1 … FOIA-5 (`_Redact.pdf`)** — Havelock PD loud-noise call records, Sept 2025 – Aug 2026: 87 calls, 6 enforcement actions (all in the first period), zero since.
- **Special_meeting-manager.pdf** — Oct. 20, 2025 Board of Commissioners minutes: the $287,415 city-manager exit package, approved in closed session.
- **US_Census_Bureau_QuickFacts_Havelock_city_NC.pdf** — per-capita income $29,099; median household $58,408; poverty rate 14.3%.
- **UC_Davis_Noise_research.pdf** — UC Davis Center for Occupational & Environmental Health: environmental noise linked to heart disease, stroke, and sleep loss.
- **PubMed-LFN_Health_effects.pdf** — peer-reviewed meta-analysis (Liang et al., 2024): low-frequency (bass) noise impairs higher-order thinking and is under-counted by standard A-weighted meters.

Every number on the site traces back to one of these.

## How it works

- A single self-contained `index.html` — all styles, scripts, and images embedded directly in the file. No build step, no framework, no dependencies.
- Hosted free on GitHub Pages.
- The contact form is handled by Web3Forms (free tier); submissions are emailed to the campaign inbox.
- `og.png` is the social-share preview image referenced by the page's Open Graph / Twitter Card tags.

## Updating the site

Edit `index.html`, commit, and GitHub Pages redeploys automatically within about a minute. (40-plus commits deep now — the record keeps growing, LOL.)
